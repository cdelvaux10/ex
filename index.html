    Common questions

    Do I need an appointment for a state inspection?

    It helps. Inspections take about an hour, and booked cars go first. We also take walk-ins when a bay is open.

    Will you call before doing extra work?

    Always. If we find something beyond what you approved, we call or text with photos and a price. Nothing extra happens without your OK.

    Can I bring my own parts?

    In most cases, yes. Labor is charged as usual, and our warranty covers labor only, not parts you supply.

    What does the warranty cover?

    Parts and labor on our repairs for 12 months or 12,000 miles, whichever comes first.

    Do you offer rides?

    Yes. We run a free shuttle anywhere in Grove City, including the college and the Premium Outlets.

    How do I pay?

    Cash, check, and all major cards. Ask us about financing for larger repairs.
